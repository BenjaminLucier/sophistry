Every chart in `@sophistry/charts`: 73 2D definitions, plus the eight 3D charts of `@sophistry/charts/3d` ([below](#3d-charts)). The table uses the component names from `@sophistry/solidjs/charts` (and `@sophistry/react/charts`); the definition behind each is the same name in lower camel case (`Chart` is `chart`, `Hist2D` is `hist2d`), except `Regression`, whose definition is `regressionPlot`.

"Needs" lists the props with no default (beyond `data`); every chart also takes `progress`, `zIndex` and `visible`, and most take `opacity`. The [Charts guide](/guides/charts/) explains the model and [Chart animation](/guides/chart-animation/) the transitions; each definition's full props are in the [API reference](/api/sophistry/charts/readme/).

Every mark with a `key` or `series` also takes `to`, `blend` and `staging` ([data transitions](/guides/chart-animation/#data-transitions)), whether or not its row lists them. `transition` is the [clock-driven update](/guides/chart-animation/#clock-driven-updates) of the per-datum marks.

Every chart is batched: per-datum marks draw as one instances node, marks with distinct outlines (lines, areas, wedges, ribbons, bands) as one path batch, and `Imshow` on linear axes as one image. There is no `rendering` option. See [Batched by default](/guides/charts/#batched-by-default).

## Bars

`BarChart` is the preset; `<Bars>` inside a `<Chart>` does the rest. `orientation="horizontal"` lays bars along a category y axis, `base` makes them float, and `layout="stack"` or `"dodge"` arranges them by `series`. Under a stack, negative values stack down from zero (diverging bars) and `offset="normalize"` makes 100% bars. `labels` writes each value past its bar, or inside it with `labels={{ position: "inside" }}`, hiding any that don't fit; `total: true` adds stack totals. `barChartHandle(node)` (and `barsHandle(node)` for `<Bars>`) gives `barBounds(i)` for annotating a bar.

## Distributions

`Density` draws a kernel density estimate; with `series`, one curve per category, and with `row`, each on its own ridgeline row. `commonNorm` scales curves by their share of the data. `orientation="horizontal"` turns it on its side, as `<JointPlot>`'s marginals do. `Rug` puts a tick at every raw value.

`Strip` spreads dots across a band by seeded jitter, so scrubbing never reshuffles them; `Beeswarm` packs them without overlap. `dodge` gives each series its own sub-band.

## Lines, scatter and error bars

`ErrorBar` takes `yError` and `xError` as one magnitude or a `[low, high]` pair. `Scatter`'s `radius` is in scene units; `size` maps a value through a square-root scale so each marker's area tracks it. `LinePlot`, `FillBetween` and `StackPlot` take `curve`: `"linear"`, `"monotone"` (smooth, never overshoots: the safe choice for data), `"natural"`, `"catmull-rom"`, or the `"step"` variants.

## Histograms

`Histogram` buckets raw values by `binCount`, or by a `range` whose step is the bin width (`[0, 10, 2]` is five bins). To re-bin across a transition, give the far end `toBinCount` or `toRange`.

## Box and violin plots

Both take one datum per group holding its raw sample in `values`. `BoxPlot` draws quartiles, whiskers at 1.5 IQR and outliers; `ViolinPlot` draws a kernel density with a median mark. With `series` and `label`, groups sharing a name stand side by side.

## Pie, polar area and radar

All three sit at `center` with `radius`. `innerRadius` makes a doughnut; `ariaLabel` names each slice for assistive technology. `RadarChart` draws one polygon per datum against shared axes; set `maxValue` to keep the scale fixed while data changes. A radar's transition joins its axes by name, so an axis can fold in or out.

## Heatmaps and contours

`Imshow` paints a matrix; `Pcolormesh` the same on a non-uniform grid; `Contour` draws iso-lines and `ContourF` filled bands; `Hist2D` and `HexBin` bin scattered points. `Tripcolor`, `TriContour` and `TriContourF` do the same for scattered points by triangulating them. Covering layers paint under the axes, whose numbers get a halo to stay readable.

## Waterfalls, funnels and candlesticks

`Waterfall` floats each change from the running total and stands `total` bars on zero. `Funnel` stacks a process's stages top-down with the loss between them shaded. `Candlestick` is a layer for price data: wicks low to high, bodies open to close.

## 3D charts

The 3D charts live in their own entry, `@sophistry/charts/3d` (components in `@sophistry/solidjs/charts/3d`), so the 2D charts never need `@sophistry/3d`. They follow the same data-and-channels conventions, render under a `camera3D` (`<Camera3D>` from `@sophistry/solidjs/3d`), and take the props of the primitive they draw (`shaded`, `stroke`, `opacity`, handlers, ...). In them `z` is a data channel; `zIndex` is still the paint order.

| Component | Needs | Notable options |
|---|---|---|
| `BarChart3D` | `x`, `y`, `z`, `height` | `width`, `depth` (default 1), `color`, `colors` |
| `Scatter3D` | `x`, `y`, `z` | `radius`, `color` |
| `Stem3D` | `x`, `y`, `z` | `baseline`, `markerRadius`, `color` |
| `Quiver3D` | `x`, `y`, `z`, `u`, `v`, `w` | `scale`, `color` |
| `SurfaceGrid3D` | `xs`, `ys`, `zs` (grids) | the mesh's paint props |
| `TriSurface3D` | `x`, `y`, `z` | the mesh's paint props |
| `Voxels3D` | `values` (a 3D grid of booleans) | the mesh's paint props |
| `Wireframe3D` | `xs`, `ys`, `zs` (grids) | the line props |

Charts of many marks (`BarChart3D`, `Quiver3D`, `Scatter3D`, `Stem3D`, `Wireframe3D`) draw one 3D primitive per datum or segment; surfaces (`SurfaceGrid3D`, `TriSurface3D`, `Voxels3D`) are one mesh each. A camera move only re-projects them. See [3D](/guides/3d/#3d-charts).
