Start here. These examples cover the pieces every scene is made of: a `<Stage>`, shapes styled by the active theme, layout helpers that place nodes by their measured bounds, a coordinate plane, and a first `draw()` and `moveTo()` driving tracks passed as props. Components come from `@sophistry/solidjs`; tracks, animations and layout helpers from `@sophistry/core`. Each is short enough to read in one sitting, and every one runs live and shows its full source.

The first three (`ShapesGallery`, `FirstSceneShapes`, `UnitCircle`) are the best starting points. `FirstScene*` examples are the steps of the [first scene tutorial](/start/first-scene/), so you can compare each step with its result.
